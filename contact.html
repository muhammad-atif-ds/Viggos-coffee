Getting here

Find us on De Coninckplein

We're at number 21 on De Coninckplein, 2060 Antwerp. When the weather is good, look for the tables and umbrella out front.

Antwerpen-Centraal station is a short walk away.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need to book a table?

No, most tables are walk-in. We hold a few for bookings on weekends, which is handy for groups of four or more.

Do you have plant-based milks?

Yes: oat, almond and soy, all at no extra cost.

Can I work from the café?

Of course. Wi-Fi is free and there are outlets along the long bar. On weekends we ask laptops to stay at the bar so tables are free for groups.

Do you cater events?

We run a pop-up espresso bar for offices, weddings and markets. Send us a message with the date and headcount and we'll send a quote.

Can I buy beans for my office?

Yes. We deliver weekly or fortnightly within the city, ground to suit your machine.
